# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 11 September 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how Seaview Apartment Xlendi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit our website, book a stay with us, or otherwise use our services.

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:
- **Personal Identifiers**: Name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
- **Booking Information**: Dates of stay, number of guests, and any special requests.
- **Payment Information**: Credit card details or other payment information, which is processed securely by our payment partners.
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use our website, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics.

### How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:
- To process your bookings and payments.
- To communicate with you about your stay.
- To improve our website and services.
- To comply with legal obligations.

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how our customers use the site. You can read more about how Google uses your Personal Information here: [https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/](https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/). You can also opt-out of Google Analytics here: [https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted partners who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

### Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [info@seaviewapartmentxlendi.com](mailto:info@seaviewapartmentxlendi.com).

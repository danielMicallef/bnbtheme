Getting to Xlendi is straightforward. Here’s a simple guide:

**From Malta to Gozo:**

1.  **Gozo Ferry:** Head to Ċirkewwa in the north of Malta. The ferry to Gozo's Mġarr Harbour leaves every 30-45 minutes and the crossing takes about 15 minutes. You can find <a href="https://www.gozochannel.com/ferry/schedule/" class="text-blue-600 visited:text-purple-600 underline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">the Gozo Channel schedule here</a>.
2.  **Fast Ferry (Passengers Only):** From Lascaris Wharf in Valletta, a 45-minute fast ferry takes you directly to Gozo. To plan your day, you can check the <a href="https://gozohighspeed.com/pages/fares-and-schedule" class="text-blue-600 visited:text-purple-600 underline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fast Ferry Schedule</a>. Please note that in case of bad weather, the fast ferry may not operate. It's always a good idea to check their <a href="https://www.facebook.com/GozoHighspeed" class="text-blue-600 visited:text-purple-600 underline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Facebook page</a> for the latest updates.

**From Mġarr Harbour (Gozo) to Xlendi:**

*   **Direct Bus:** The TD30 bus goes directly from the harbour to Xlendi, running every 30 minutes on weekdays.
*   **Connecting Buses:** Alternatively, take bus 301 to Victoria (Rabat), then switch to bus 306 to reach Xlendi.
